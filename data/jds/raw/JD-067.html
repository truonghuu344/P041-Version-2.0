      Company: MyStorage — Vietnam's first tech-enabled self-storage company

      Location: Ho Chi Minh City (on-site, with flexibility)

      Type: Internship, 3 months · Full-time (Mon–Fri)

      Team: Technology

      Allowance: 5,000,000 VND gross/month

      After the internship: Opportunity to convert to a full-time role

      Why this role exists

      Most internships give you a sandbox project that gets deleted when you leave. This one doesn't.

      MyStorage runs a real business — self-storage, valet storage, hyperlocal lockers, and wine storage — serving thousands of customers across Vietnam, Korea, and Japan. Our tech team is small and the backlog is not. Our AI Sales Agent, which handles customer conversations and helps drive our sales pipeline, has won regional awards for technology and innovation.

      We're looking for one intern who can take a problem, build the thing, and ship it to production — with Claude Code as their primary engineering tool. Your work will be used by real customers and real colleagues within weeks of joining.

      Three months full-time is enough to do this properly: a couple of weeks to get your bearings and ship your first small things, then the rest of the time owning real work end to end. Do it well and there's a full-time role waiting.

      What you'll actually do
      • Ship features to production. Customer-facing pages on our website (modern web stack), internal dashboards, and booking flows. Real PRs, real code review, real deploys.
      • Build internal tools that remove manual work. Our Sales, Marketing, and Operations teams still do things by hand that shouldn't be done by hand. You'll find those tasks and automate them — workflow automation, CRM integrations, internal bots and dashboards.
      • Work on our AI Sales Agent. Prompt design, evaluation, tool integrations, and the plumbing that connects an AI agent to a live sales pipeline.
      • Wire up data. Analytics tracking, data pipelines, and turning messy operational data into something a human can make a decision from.
      • Prototype fast. When someone on the team says "I wish we had X," you should be able to have a working version in front of them the same week.
      How we work (and what "AI-native" means here)

      Claude Code is our default way of building. That does not mean prompting until something compiles. It means:

      • You write a clear spec before you write code — because a vague spec produces vague software, whether a human or a model writes it.
      • You read every line the model produces and you can defend it in review. If you can't explain it, it doesn't ship.
      • You know when to reach for AI and when to just write the code yourself. Both are correct answers in different situations.
      • You test. Generated code that looks right and is wrong is worse than no code at all.

      We care far more about how much real product you can ship than about how many years of experience you have.

      What we're looking for

      Required

      • Available full-time for 3 consecutive months. This role suits final-year students and recent graduates best — but if you're an earlier-year student who can genuinely commit five days a week for three months, we want to hear from you too
      • Final-year student, recent graduate, or self-taught builder — degree not required
      • Comfortable with JavaScript/TypeScript or Python, and Git
      • You've built and shipped something outside of coursework. A side project, a bot, an automation, a tool for a friend's business — anything with real users, even five of them
      • Genuine hands-on experience with AI coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot) and honest opinions about where they help and where they don't
      • Working English (our docs and codebase are in English; the team speaks Vietnamese day-to-day)
      • You ask questions early instead of quietly getting stuck for three days

      Nice to have

      • Next.js, React, or WordPress
      • SQL, BigQuery, or any data warehouse
      • Experience with LLM APIs, RAG, agents, or MCP
      • Automation platforms (n8n, Zapier, Make, or similar)
      • An eye for design — you notice when something looks wrong
      What you get
      • Ownership of shipped features, with your name on them, that you can point to in every future interview
      • Direct mentorship from our engineering team, plus exposure to how Commercial, Marketing, and Operations actually make decisions
      • A full Claude Code license and the tooling budget to work properly
      • A serious conversion path. We hire from this internship. Ship well over three months and we'll make you a full-time offer
      • Small-company leverage. You'll sit close enough to the business to see the effect of what you build on revenue
