      Tinovation is a technology company focusing on real products and real projects.

      We highly value self-discipline, independence, and proactiveness in our working culture.

      We are currently looking for 01 NodeJS Intern / Fresher Developer who wants to grow in a practical tech environment.

      JOB DETAILS

      • Position: NodeJS Intern / Fresher Develope
      • Openings: 01
      • Location: 27 Street No.4, Khang An Residential Area, Phu Huu Ward, Thu Duc City, Ho Chi Minh City

      Job Description

      • Participate in backend development using NodeJS (ExpressJS / NestJS).
      • Build and integrate RESTful APIs.
      • Work with databases such as MongoDB or SQL-based systems.
      • Join real projects with real workflows (Git, code review, task management).
      • Collaborate with frontend, QA, and other team members.

      Requirements

      • Final-year student or fresh graduate in Computer Science / Software Engineering or related fields.
      • Basic knowledge of: JavaScript & NodeJS, HTTP, RESTful APIs, Databases.
      • Strong self-learning ability, self-discipline, and proactive mindset.
      • Responsible for assigned tasks.
      • Familiar with Git.
      • Experience with ExpressJS, NestJS, or personal projects is a plus.

      Who Will Fit Well

      • Those who want hands-on experience, not just theory.
      • Can learn and solve problems independently.
      • Aim to grow into a Junior / Middle NodeJS Developer.

      Benefits

      • Internship allowance / fresher salary based on capability.
      • Technical mentorship and code review.
      • Opportunity for full-time employment after internship.
      • Lean, open, and performance-focused environment.
      • Team activities and company events.

      CONTACT

      • Email: tino@tinovation.io.vn (Email subject: [Tinovation] NodeJS Intern/Fresher - Your Name)
      • Phone: 08 2727 7157 hoặc 0708 357 077
      • Zalo: 08 2727 7157

      Tinovation looks forward to finding the right teammate to grow together and build outstanding software products!
