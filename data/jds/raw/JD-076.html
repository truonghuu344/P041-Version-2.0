      Job Description

      Job Purpose & Dimensions:

      We are looking for an engineer to maintain and enhance a design data management platform that supports product development, documentation, and engineering collaboration.

      This role covers application development, platform operations, release support, user enablement, and integration with related engineering systems.

      Principal Accountabilities:

      • Develop and maintain application features based on engineering and business requirements.
      • Support software releases, testing, deployment, and user communication.
      • Administer platform infrastructure, environments, and system performance.
      • Plan and execute system upgrades, migrations, and service improvements.
      • Implement monitoring, troubleshooting, and reliability practices.
      • Provide user support, training, and clear technical documentation.
      • Integrate the platform with other tools and services in the engineering environment.
      • Collaborate with stakeholders to prioritize improvements and support adoption.

      Key Performance Measures:

      • Platform reliability, performance, and service availability.
      • Timely delivery of planned releases and improvements.
      • Successful execution of upgrades and migration activities.
      • Effective monitoring, issue resolution, and continuous improvement.
      • Positive user feedback and strong adoption of the platform.
      • Ability to learn new technologies and adapt to evolving needs.
      • Strong collaboration and contribution to team goals.

      Knowledge, Skills and Experience:

      • Experience in full-stack application development on ASP.NET + IIS, C# & C++ programming languages and database-backed systems.
      • Strong database knowledge, including query development and performance tuning.
      • Experience with server administration and application hosting environments.
      • Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and deployment concepts.
      • Experience with monitoring, diagnostics, and incident response practices.
      • Proficiency in scripting and automation for operational tasks.
      • Understanding of release management, testing, and deployment workflows.
      • Knowledge of infrastructure, networking, and migration planning.
      • Familiarity with project tracking and Agile ways of working.
      • Comfortable working in cross-functional teams and iterative delivery models.
      • Interest in modern development tools and continuous improvement.
      • Knowledge of APIs and system integration patterns is a plus.
      • Experience in engineering, technical documentation, or data management domains is beneficial.
      • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.





      Qualifications



      • Solid understanding of modern software development practices and C#, C++ VB.NET or similar programming languages.
      • Bachelor or Master degree with relevant background (Computer Science, IT, Engineering, or related).
      • Minimum 3–5 years of professional experience in application development and/or infrastructure engineering.
      • Business fluent in English.

      Key Relationships:

      • Partner with engineering users and stakeholders to gather needs and feedback.
      • Collaborate with product, infrastructure, and operations teams.
      • Work with technical specialists to support hosting and service reliability.
      • Engage with users to support adoption, training, and continuous improvement.
      • Coordinate across teams to balance service quality, priorities, and delivery.
      • Contribute to a collaborative and knowledge-sharing team culture.

      Additional Information

      Renesas is an embedded semiconductor solution provider driven by its Purpose ‘To Make Our Lives Easier.’ As the industry’s leading expert in embedded processing with unmatched quality and system-level know-how, we have evolved to provide scalable and comprehensive semiconductor solutions for automotive, industrial, infrastructure, and IoT industries based on the broadest product portfolio, including High Performance Computing, Embedded Processing, Analog & Connectivity, and Power.



      With a diverse team of over 21,000 professionals in more than 30 countries, we continue to expand our boundaries to offer enhanced user experiences through digitalization and usher into a new era of innovation. We design and develop sustainable, power-efficient solutions today that help people and communities thrive tomorrow, ‘To Make Our Lives Easier.’



      At Renesas, you can:

      • Launch and advance your career in technical and business roles across four Product Groups and various corporate functions. You will have the opportunities to explore our hardware and software capabilities and try new things.
      • Make a real impact by developing innovative products and solutions to meet our global customers' evolving needs and help make people’s lives easier, safe and secure.
      • Maximize your performance and wellbeing in our flexible and inclusive work environment. Our people-first culture and global support system, including the remote work option and Employee Resource Groups, will help you excel from the first day.
