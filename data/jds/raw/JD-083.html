      Trusting Social is an AI Fintech pioneer that's revolutionizing credit access in emerging markets. Our mission is "Advancing AI to Meet the Financial Needs of Everyday Consumers with Empathy." We've assessed over 1 billion consumers across four countries, and we're on a mission to provide 100 million credit lines using the power of AI and Big Data.

      How You'll Make an Impact

      Join Trusting Social as a Full-Stack AI Engineer II and help build products that are transforming financial services in Vietnam and beyond. You will contribute to our Co-Lending partnership initiatives, building systems across the full stack (backend, web and mobile) that enable fast, scalable credit evaluation and secure integration with banks, partners, and third-party platforms.

      Your work will help power instant registration, real-time credit approval, and automated decisioning at scale. As an Engineer II, you deliver features end-to-end within a defined scope with increasing autonomy, working AI-native and broadening your skills across the full stack as you grow

      What You'll Do

      • Deliver high-quality features end-to-end across the full stack (backend, web, mobile), working with cross-functional teams and ensuring systems are reliable, highly scalable, and fault-tolerant.
      • Work AI-native, orchestrating agents: frame the problem for the agent, delegate the implementation, run multiple agent sessions per day, and spend your own time on direction, quality review, and the hard edge cases.
      • Build and maintain the tests, evals, and harnesses that agents depend on, so the system stays legible and reliable.
      • Follow Agile/DevOps and CI/CD to deliver quality code at speed.
      • Identify and resolve technical challenges within your scope with increasing autonomy.
      • Support and mentor new joiners, and share knowledge that raises team productivity.
      • Continuously learn and stay current with advances in software engineering and AI technologies.
      • Perform other work duties as assigned by the line manager.

      Beyond the core

      These Are The Dimensions We Grow Our Engineers Into — And It's a Plus If You Already Operate In One Or More Of Them

      • Domain expert & architect: learn a relevant partner/product area and encode its rules into reusable skills and docs that others and AI agents can reuse; contribute product and design judgment, not just implementation.
      • Platform & operations owner: operate and monitor agent-assisted systems in production, holding the bar on reliability, security, and quality at the integration boundary.
      • Partner-facing engineer: manage trust at the partner/client boundary within your scope, and communicate clearly across functions.

      What We're Looking For

      • AI-native delivery: a track record of (or clear eagerness for) shipping production work by orchestrating AI coding agents — running multiple agent sessions, directing and reviewing their output, and building the tests and harnesses that keep it reliable.
      • Full-stack engineering: solid technical foundation with 4+ years of software engineering across backend and frontend (web or mobile); experience building distributed systems, banking, or fintech is a plus.
      • Security-aware by default: you keep data protection, access control, and auditability in mind, and are comfortable working within banking/partner compliance constraints.
      • Strong sense of ownership and accountability for product quality and delivery timelines.
      • A collaborative team player who contributes positively to the team environment.
      • Proactive, open-minded, and quick to learn.
      • Motivated to raise the bar and continuously improve products and engineering practices.
      • Good communication skills with working proficiency in English.

      What We Offer

      Join Our Vibrant Team And Enjoy

      • Opportunity to work and learn from one of the best and brightest technology teams in Vietnam
      • Be part of a winning team with exponential growth regionally, experience recruiting world-class talents
      • Competitive compensation package, including 13th-month salary and performance bonuses
      • Comprehensive health care coverage for you and your dependents
      • Generous leave policies, including annual leave, sick leave, and flexible work hours
      • Convenient central district 1 office location, next to a future metro station
      • Onsite lunch with multiple options, including vegetarian
      • Grab for work allowance and fully equipped workstations
      • Fun and engaging team building activities, sponsored sports clubs, and happy hour every Thursday
      • Unlimited free coffee, tea, snacks, and fruit to keep you energized
      • An opportunity to make a social impact by helping to democratize credit access in emerging markets

      At Trusting Social, we live by ownership, integrity, and agility in execution. We believe in doing what's right, what's best, and what's innovative. If you're smart, driven, and want to make a difference in the world with the most advanced and fascinating technology, come join our team. We offer the runway to truly make an impact.
