      Mai Anh

      Mai Anh

      Gene Solutions is Vietnam's leading biotechnology company, developing AI-powered precision medicine solutions and genomic diagnostics across Asia. Our Oncology team builds intelligent products that support clinicians in delivering faster, more accurate cancer care. We are looking for motivated Product Engineer Interns who want to build impactful healthcare products and grow into future Product Engineers.

      About the Role

      As a Product Engineer Intern, you will work closely with software engineers, AI scientists, clinicians, and product teams to develop and improve Oncology products used in real clinical workflows. You will receive hands-on, on-the-job coaching through real projects and be mentored by PhD-level scientists and senior AI engineers.

      Key Responsibilities

      • Support the development and maintenance of Oncology products and AI-powered applications.
      • Collaborate with engineers, clinicians, and product teams to improve product quality and user experience.
      • Validate AI outputs and clinical knowledge to improve accuracy.
      • Test new features, investigate issues, and propose product improvements.
      • Document findings and contribute to product development.

      What We're Looking For

      Behaviors (Most Important)

      • Extreme Ownership – Take responsibility and follow through.
      • Bias Toward Delivery – Focus on delivering value.
      • Learn Fast, Learn Always – Curious and eager to learn.
      • Clarity of Intent – Communicate clearly and ask questions.
      • Act Now, Not Later – Be proactive and execution-oriented.

      Qualifications

      • Final-year undergraduate or graduate student in Computer Science, AI, Bioinformatics, Data Science, Software Engineering, or related fields.
      • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
      • Technologies you will work with:
      • Development: Python, REST APIs, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, ClickHouse, Airflow, Minio, Docker, Git, CI/CD.
      • AI: LLMs, AI Agents, RAG, Prompt Engineering, Model Evaluation.
      • Available to work on-site at Gene Solutions (Ho Chi Minh city office) for at least 2 full days per week.

      Why Join This Internship

      • Outstanding interns will be considered for a full-time Product Engineer position.
      • Work directly with PhD-level scientists, senior engineers, and product leaders.
      • Learn by building real healthcare products.
      • Take ownership of meaningful projects instead of only supporting routine tasks. Contribute to AI-powered oncology products used in clinical practice across Asia.
      • Internship lasts 4 months, with performance reviews every 2 months and continuous feedback.
      • Allowance: 5mil/month
