      WEALLNET is a brand new experience agency that exists to reimagine what an experience can be. We do that by pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in every format — virtual, live or hybrid. Born out of smart insights and built creatively with a content-first approach, our vision is imaginative and engaging, innovative beyond expectation, and flawlessly executed. Each and every day we are driven to do one thing: Live Extraordinary.

      At the core of our agency is our people - a dedicated team of engineers, creative innovators, world-class futurists, operational experts and business partners. And we’re looking for motivated, ambitious team players to join us.

      We are looking to hire a tech whizz who’s keen to champion technology at our lovely HCMC headquarters.

      Candidate Requirements

      • Fresh graduates in Computer Science, Software Engineering and passion about technology development, constantly seeking, researching to learn and improving
      • Good Knowledge in Object-oriented programming concept
      • Knowledge or work experiences in any of the following programing languages or technologies: Golang, SQL, JavaScript, Flutter, NEXT.JS, Python, etc
      • Breath in of new trends and best practices in development
      • Good Analytical and communication skills
      • Proficiency in understanding of cross-browser compatibility issues
      • Up-to-date experience with protocols, standards, and technologies
      • Having an experience in algorithm is a plus
      • Knowledge of project tools like Jira, Git, etc is a plus
      • Able to work independently, in a work group or under pressure
      • Ability to work in the office full time.
      • Ability to listen and learn
      • In love with social media, news, digital communication, e-commerce and payment
      • Must be able to communicate effectively in English (IELTS 7.0)

      Candidate Internship’s Rights

      • Flexible 6 months or 1 year internship program option
      • General amenities from office
      • Allow tea-break and attend company’s catering events
      • Opportunity to work with a creative team at a cool new office in downtown district 1
      • Opportunity to network and learn from the best in the technology field
      • Chance to gain invaluable real-life work experience working after 6 months’ experience
      • Attend company’s teambuilding and outdoor events
      • Attend company’s workshop to advance career path
      • Obtain signature, stamp and letter of recommendation upon completion of internship.
      • Opportunity to get permanent job offer if qualified

      The Ideal Candidate

      • You have a knack for digital thinking and constantly research tech trends, demonstrated by your development experience
      • You have knowledge in providing quick ballparks and accurate estimates, clearly defining the scope and underlying assumptions
      • Awareness of latest industry developments and trends
      • As a start-up company we expect you to go above and beyond what is required on the JD. Be proactive and embrace challenges as opportunities. There may times occasionally where you may be asked to stay back or come in on weekends

      What kind of person are you?

      • You have a warm positive attitude and don’t mind motivating others
      • Good people skills for the difficult moments as well as the easy ones
      • You have proven tech-savvy skills
      • You have proven understanding of our business or very similar ones
      • You can often foresee upcoming issues and can discuss fairly with your leader
      • You are unfazed by a little pressure and enjoy the energy of a team
