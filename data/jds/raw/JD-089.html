      YOUR NEW ROLE CODE LEAP

      As a ReactJS Engineer intern at CODE LEAP, you will be integrated into a team where you will work on significant projects to enhance our products and streamline our services. This internship is designed to provide a comprehensive learning experience, enabling you to acquire practical skills that align with your career objectives.

      YOUR EXCITING TASKS

      • Develop and maintain software components under the supervision of senior engineers
      • Implement and test new features following coding standards and practices
      • Assist in debugging and refining application functionality
      • Collaborate with the development team to discuss and solve problems
      • Participate in software design sessions and contribute fresh ideas to enhance user experience
      • Gain exposure to software architecture and project management processes
      • Assist in the documentation of software functionality

      YOUR PROFILE AND YOUR PERSONALITY

      • Enrollment in an undergraduate or graduate program in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
      • Demonstrated passion for technology and innovation through coursework, academic projects, internships, or personal initiatives involving ReactJS
      • A foundational understanding of algorithms, data structures, and software design principles
      • Basic understanding of JavaScript/TypeScript fundamentals and modern frontend frameworks such as ReactJS
      • Experience with development tools and technologies (e.g., Git, version control systems)
      • Strong problem-solving skills and a keen attention to detail.
      • Eager to learn new technologies and frameworks.
      • Proactively reach out to people, communicate, ask for help, and get things done
      • Proficiency in English (at least from 6.5 IELTS or CEFR B2)
      • Available to join the internship at least 4 days per week

      Nice to have: Previous internship experience in software development is a plus

      Benefits Await You At Code Leap

      • Real-world experience in software development within a fast-paced industry
      • Mentorship from seasoned software engineers
      • Allowance will be provided
      • Enjoy a stress-free and comfortable commute to and from work with our Grab sponsorship
      • Enjoy an endless selection of delicious refreshments, including gourmet coffee, beverages, snacks, and fresh fruits, all complimentary
      • Focused on individual development:
        • Unlimited access to Udemy and other recommendation courses
        • Sponsored Professional certificates such as Front End Development Libraries Certification, Modern React with Redux, etc.
        • Sharing session every Friday, come along with lunch provided by CODE LEAP
      • Offers days off to align with personal university schedules and commitments
      • Dynamic, fun, and agile environment, perfect for sharing and creativity
      • Open-minded, kind & humble colleagues
      CONTACT

      Please submit your CV and a brief personal introduction via our recruitment email: chau.luu@codeleap.de.

      Additionally, let us know your preference: coffee or tea?

      We're looking forward to your application!
