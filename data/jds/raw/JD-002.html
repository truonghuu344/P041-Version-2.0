      Bouygues Construction IT Vietnam provided pay range

      This range is provided by Bouygues Construction IT Vietnam. Your actual pay will be based on your skills and experience — talk with your recruiter to learn more.

      Base pay range

      ₫6,000,000/mo - ₫6,600,000/mo
      Bouygues Construction IT Vietnam is a part of Bouygues Construction IT, a trusted partner for the business lines which provides competitiveness of the Bouygues Construction Group and promotes its digital transformation.

      In the software development service, we will provide solutions for our collaborator from Bouygues Construction Group with various web and mobile applications which support their daily works in different business scopes: purchasing, communication, finance, … By this position, your key responsibilities will be:

      • Design, and develop new features in both Front-end and Back-end sites
      • Learn development framework, perform design and coding
      • Perform test applications and system
      • Make plans and control the work progress as scheduled

      Our Requirements

      • Standard development knowledge, skill in Java, PHP, or other programming languages
      • Basic Front-end programming skills with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, especially is Angular
      • Good knowledge and skill in using Unit test (Junit, JMock, …)
      • Good knowledge of Design Pattern
      • Understanding of the software development process
      • Good at English. French is a big plus

      General Skills

      • Good communication skills
      • Good problem-solving and research skills
      • Energetic and eager to tackle new projects and ideas
      • Well-organized and can-do attitude
      • Good mindset, positive thinking

      Our Benefits

      Professional, Open-minded and Creative Environment:

      • International, friendly, proactive, supportive workplace.
      • Great teamwork with Agile mindset.
      • Fostering mutual respect and understanding.
      • Strong sharing culture to improve individual development.

      Individual Development

      • Extensive training and in-depth knowledge sharing sessions.
      • Online internal learning hub with various categories in hard skills & soft skills (languages, programming language, data visualization…).
      • Individual empowerment.

      Special Care

      For Intern:

      • Attractive monthly internship allowance.
      • Dell laptop and external monitor for your work.
      • Premium health insurance.
      • 1 paid leave day per month.

      For Full-time Employee

      • Work from home 2 days/ week.
      • 100% salary on probationary period.
      • Performance bonus.
      • 15 annual leave days + 6 sick leave days (plus 1 annual leave day for every 3-year working).
      • Annual health check-up and premium health insurance for employee (and your beloved children after 3-working year).
      • Various attractive teambuilding activities and company trip.
      • Sport, personal activities sponsor.

        Entry level

      • Job function

        Engineering and Information Technology

      • Industries

        IT Services and IT Consulting
