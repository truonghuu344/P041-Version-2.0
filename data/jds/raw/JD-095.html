      Job Details

      Job Description:

      A Design Verification (DV) Intern supports pre-silicon validation by developing testbenches, running simulations, and debugging digital ICs. They collaborate closely with RTL designers to ensure functional correctness and achieve verification closure, gaining hands-on exposure to industry-standard methodologies.

      Key Responsibilities

      • Testbench Development: Assist in developing and enhancing constrained-random verification components (e.g., sequences, monitors, scoreboards) using SystemVerilog and UVM (Universal Verification Methodology).
      • Simulation & Debugging: Execute regression tests, analyze waveform viewers, check coverage metrics (code/functional), and debug RTL simulation failures.
      • Automation & Scripting: Write and maintain automation scripts using Python, Perl, or Tcl to improve the efficiency of the verification flow.
      • Documentation: Maintain clear documentation of test plans, coverage results, and bug reports.

      Qualifications

      • Third-year or Final-year student currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Computer Science or a closely related field (Physics, Maths).
      • Be able to work onsite for 4 days per week.
      • Be able to commit to at least a 6-month internship program.
      • Strong verbal and written communication skills in English.

      Technical Skills

      • Strong foundational knowledge of digital logic design and Verilog/SystemVerilog.
      • Familiarity with Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) and languages like C/C++ or Python.
      • Basic understanding of UVM concepts, SVA (SystemVerilog Assertions), and IC design flows is highly preferred.

      Soft Skills: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, paired with a collaborative team spirit and a passion for continuous learning.

      Benefit Package of Interns at Intel Vietnam

      • Attractive internship allowance
      • Training programs, opportunities to be involved in multinational projects.
      • Exciting extracurricular and sports programs for employees
      • Well-invested facilities, putting safety first.
      • Shuttle bus system at many places in Ho Chi Minh City, Binh Duong, Dong Nai.
      • High-quality cafeteria, lunch allowance.
      • Safe, stable and dynamic working environment.
